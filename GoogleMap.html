NTU – Google Map Review Analysis



This project was conducted as part of the NTU Business Analytics curriculum. Its main objective was to analyze unstructured Google Map reviews to extract structured consumer insights, using advanced sentiment analysis and network centrality techniques. The results provided a clear understanding of what consumers value most (e.g., taste, ambiance, service) when evaluating non-chain cafés and restaurants.

My Contribution



  • Built an aspect-based sentiment analysis pipeline using PyABSA and FAST_LCF_ATEPC, transforming unorganized reviews into structured datasets of consumer sentiment
  • Collaborated with teammates to apply eigenvector centrality analysis on review aspects, identifying and ranking the most influential factors shaping customer satisfaction
  • Delivered data-driven insights on consumer preferences, enabling clearer prioritization of features that drive positive or negative reviews
